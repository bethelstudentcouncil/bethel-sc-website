Our mission

Bethel International School aims to develop global citizens who are competent, responsible, possessing an international perspective and equipped with values grounded on biblical truths to serve as leaders and models of change in an interdependent world.

Our vision

Preparing Godly leaders for the modern world.

Students leading students

The people behind the pep rallies, the polls, and the programs.

The Bethel International School Student Council represents the student body across Preschool through Senior High. We plan events, carry student concerns to the administration, and build the kind of school spirit that makes Bethel feel like home.

Every officer here was elected by students, for students — and every program we run starts with one question: what does Bethel need this year?
